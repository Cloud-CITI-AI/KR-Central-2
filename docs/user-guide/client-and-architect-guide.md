# Client and architect guide

For people KR Surveys has invited to follow a subdivision job. You see only the jobs you were given, and what you may
change depends on your role.

## Getting access

1. Your KR Surveys contact adds your **email address** and your role (client or architect) for your job.
2. If you do not already have a Microsoft sign-in that KR Surveys recognises, you receive an invitation from Microsoft on
   behalf of KR Surveys. Accept it. If the invitation asks you to sign in or
   create an account, use the **same email address** KR Surveys has on file.
3. Open the tracker link KR Surveys sent you and choose **Sign in with Microsoft**. The first time you sign in, your job
   appears. If the page says "You don't have access to any jobs yet — ask your KR Surveys contact." the address did not
   match or the job is not set up yet: tell your contact which address you used.

If you see "We couldn't confirm your email address – ask your KR Surveys contact.", your Microsoft account has an address
Microsoft has not verified; your contact will arrange another way in.

You are signed out after 2 hours without activity or 12 hours in total. Use **Sign out** on a shared computer.

## Reading your job

- The **dashboard** lists your jobs with a progress bar and a **NEXT TO ACTION** box: what is happening next and who is
  responsible (KR Surveys, Client, Architect). **Open job →** opens the tracker.
- The tracker shows **10 stages**; click a bar to open it. Each checkpoint is **Completed** (green), **Not completed**
  (red) or **Not required** (grey), with the word on the card as well as the colour.
- Agency stages can be greyed out: "Not required – no conditions in the Decision Letter". That is normal.
- The summary shows overall progress, the WAPC approval date and expiry, and, once known, a countdown to the WAPC outcome date.
- **Save as PDF** expands every stage and opens your browser's print dialog; choose "Save as PDF" there for a copy.

## What you can do

| You can                                                                                   | Client | Architect                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------- |
| See your job, its stages, dates and files                                                 | Yes    | Yes (not the fee proposal)       |
| See the fee proposal                                                                      | Yes    | No                               |
| Approve or decline a proposal or quote (a reason is needed to decline)                    | Yes    | No                               |
| Press the payment or completion button on your checkpoints (for example **INVOICE PAID**) | Yes    | No                               |
| Upload the completed letter of consent, a Notice of Completion or plumber documents       | Yes    | No                               |
| Upload evidence for a WAPC condition and write in the **Client comment** box              | Yes    | Yes                              |
| Add **JOB NOTES**                                                                         | Yes    | Yes                              |
| Download **DOWNLOAD ALL FILES (.ZIP)**                                                    | Yes    | Yes (without fee proposal files) |

If a banner says "You have view-only access to this tracker, so changes can't be saved.", that is how architects use most
cards. Anything you add can be removed by you, and KR Surveys staff can remove any file.

## Approving, paying and uploading

- **Approve or decline.** On a proposal or quote choose the green button, add a comment if you wish, and confirm. To
  decline, write why first. A result box shows what you decided; **Change** reopens it. A decision tells KR Surveys; they
  update the checkpoint status.
- **Payment buttons.** Press the button when you have paid (for example **INVOICE PAID**). It records the time; **Undo** takes it back.
- **Upload.** Drag a file onto the dashed box or click it. Photos and PDFs are accepted where the box says so, up to 50 MB.
  A file shows **Scanning…** for a few seconds while it is checked for viruses. If it shows **Blocked – failed the virus
  scan**, it was rejected: check the file on your own device and contact KR Surveys. If it shows **Couldn't be checked for
  viruses – remove it and upload it again**, remove it with the × and **Yes**, and upload it again.
- **Conditions.** Under 5.0 you can read each WAPC condition, add your own comment, and drop evidence (photos, a statutory
  declaration, approvals) into **Drop clearance evidence here**. Comments save by themselves.

## Your information

The tracker holds the job details KR Surveys needs: names, email addresses, site addresses and the documents on the job.
Everything you change is recorded with your name. Each file is checked for viruses, and a job is shown only to the people
KR Surveys has given access. Ask KR Surveys for its privacy notice, or what is held about you.

## Getting help

Contact your KR Surveys contact. Tell them the job number, what you were doing, what the page said and the time. If an
error page shows a **request id**, include it. Do not email passwords or other people's documents.
